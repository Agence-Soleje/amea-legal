1Data controller

Améa is published by Agence Soleje, the controller of your data. Contact for any question about your data: contact@agencesoleje.com. Améa is for people aged 18 and over. We never sell your data, show no advertising and use no advertising tracking or audience measurement tools.

2Data we collect

Account: email address, password (encrypted, never readable), first name, nickname, date of birth.

Couple profile: relationship start date, status, living situation, wedding date, children (first names and dates of birth) if you enter them.

Couple content: planned and completed dates, photos, captions, answers to games, daily questions and the Lab, moods and check-ins, shared lists, dreams and dream map pins, anniversaries, challenges, avatar.

Location: only if you tick “Add the place” when sending a photo, or if you place a dream on the map.

Technical use: app version, phone system and version, date of last opening; notification token if you turn notifications on; your consent choices (artificial intelligence, notifications).

Error log: when the app crashes, the technical message, the screen concerned and a randomly drawn device identifier, with no link to your account and no content.

Referrals: the code shared and the resulting sign-up.

Subscription: the couple's subscription status (plan, expiry date); the payment itself is handled by Apple or Google, we never see your bank details.

Usage log: a few dated events, with no content at all — sign-up, linking and unlinking of the couple, first answer to a daily question, first check-in, first planned date, opening of the app (once a day), of the paywall and of a notification, Premium subscription. It is used to measure activation and retention, as aggregated figures.

3Purposes and legal bases

Providing the app and its couple features (account, profile, content, sharing with your partner, subscription): performance of the contract (Terms of Use), Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Checking the minimum age of 18: performance of the contract.

Artificial intelligence features: your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), obtained before the first transfer and withdrawable at any time.

Place of a photo: your consent, given photo by photo.

Notifications: your consent, given in your phone settings.

Deletion of inactive accounts: the obligation not to keep data longer than necessary (Article 5(1)(e) GDPR).

Error log, technical information and support: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working (Article 6(1)(f)).

Some answers may touch on your intimate life: you are free to answer them or not, only your partner can see them and their content is never passed on to a third party.

Usage log: our legitimate interest in understanding, as aggregated figures, what helps couples use Améa (Article 6(1)(f)). You can object by writing to contact@agencesoleje.com.

4Artificial intelligence

Two features rely on OpenAI (OpenAI Ireland Ltd, with processing in the United States), only if you have agreed.

The date planner receives your mood, your partner's mood (only if they have agreed too), the weather you enter, your budget and the length you choose, to suggest a date plan.

The “For you” suggestions receive a couple profile with no names: status, years together, whether you have children and how many, living situation, compatibility scores by game theme, number of dates, check-ins and daily questions, only if you have both agreed.

Never sent: your name, your email, your photos, the text of your answers and your messages. Under its API terms, OpenAI does not use this data to train its models and keeps it for at most 30 days for abuse prevention. You can withdraw your agreement in My account › Artificial intelligence: both features are then turned off.

5Service providers and recipients

Your couple content can only be seen by you and your partner. It is never public.

Our service providers, who process data on our behalf and on our instructions:

• Supabase (hosting of the database, photos and authentication, including account emails) — servers in Frankfurt, Germany;

• OpenAI (artificial intelligence features, see above);

• Expo (delivery of notifications: notification token and notification text), via Apple (APNs) and Google (FCM);

• Brevo (France, data hosted in the European Union): sending the email that warns you before an inactive account is deleted (email address, first name, language);

• RevenueCat (subscription management: couple identifier and purchase receipts sent by Apple or Google);

• Apple and Google (app distribution, subscription payments, notifications);

• OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (searching for a place on the dream map: the text searched or the point tapped, sent from your phone);

• Apple Maps (iPhone) and Google Maps (Android) for displaying maps;

• Pexels and Unsplash, which host some illustration images: when displaying them, your phone contacts their servers, which see your IP address.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

6Transfers outside the European Union

The database and photos are hosted in the European Union (AWS, Frankfurt). When data leaves the European Union, the transfer is covered as follows:

• OpenAI Ireland Ltd (Ireland): transfers to the United States covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses;

• Expo (650 Industries, Inc., United States): EU–US Data Privacy Framework (active participant) and standard contractual clauses;

• RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): standard contractual clauses;

• Apple Distribution International Ltd (Ireland), transfers to Apple Inc. (United States): standard contractual clauses;

• Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC: Data Privacy Framework (active participant) and standard contractual clauses;

• Supabase Pte. Ltd (Singapore) and its sub-processors, for any access from outside the EU (support, operations): standard contractual clauses;

• Brevo (France): data hosted in the EU; its own sub-processors outside the EU are covered by the Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses;

• Pexels (Canva Germany GmbH, Germany): established in the EU;

• Unsplash Inc. (Canada): European Commission adequacy decision for Canada;

• OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom): European Commission adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

You can obtain a copy of the applicable safeguards by writing to contact@agencesoleje.com.

7Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. An account that has not been opened for 2 years is deleted, exactly as if you had asked for it; an email warns you 30 days before, and simply opening the app keeps your account. Deleting your account (My account › Delete my account) is immediate and permanent: there is no waiting period and no way back. Your account, profile, avatar and personal data (answers to games, daily questions and the Lab, moods, check-ins, notification preferences) are erased at once, along with the photos you added to dates, which also disappear for your partner. If you were in a couple, your partner keeps the couple and what you built together: dates (including the ones you created, which no longer show your name), lists, dreams, challenges and progress. If you were alone in the couple, the whole couple and all its photos are erased.

Other periods: error log, 90 days; technical information (version, last opening), replaced at each opening and erased with the account; data sent to OpenAI, at most 30 days at OpenAI; administration log, which only contains the account identifier and the type of action (never your content), kept for 3 years, including after the account is deleted, for the security of the service; automatic database backups, erased as they rotate by our hosting provider.

A subscription taken out on the App Store or Google Play is not cancelled by deleting your account: cancel it in the store settings.

Usage log: 13 months, then automatically erased; also erased with the account.

8Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data, and to withdraw any consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

In the app: edit your profile in My account; delete your account in My account › Delete my account; withdraw your agreement for artificial intelligence in My account › Artificial intelligence; manage notifications in My account and in your phone settings.

For any other request: contact@agencesoleje.com. We reply within 30 days.

You may also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or with the authority of your country.

9Security

Exchanges between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Photos are stored in private spaces, accessible only to your couple through temporary links. Access to data is limited by security rules in the database: each couple only sees its own content. Data and photos are also encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, Supabase.

10Minors

Améa is for people aged 18 and over: age is checked at sign-up. Your children's first names and dates of birth, if you choose to enter them, are only used for your couple profile; they are not passed on to any provider, OpenAI included (only whether you have children and how many may be, with your agreement).
